The complete formula

Move from a rate card to an outcome.

1. Stack cost / minute
platform + telephony + STT + TTS + LLM

2. Monthly AI spend
(calls × minutes × stack rate) + fixed fees

3. Human handoff cost
unresolved calls × handoff minutes × hourly cost ÷ 60

4. Loaded cost / resolution
(AI spend + handoffs) ÷ AI-resolved calls

Decision rules

Separate fixed and variable.
A subscription changes effective per-minute cost at every volume.

Measure real resolution.
Transfers, repeats, and incorrect completion are not automated outcomes.

Count cleanup.
Human escalation belongs in the operating cost, not in a footnote.

How do you calculate voice AI cost per minute?

Add the per-minute platform, telephony, STT, TTS, and LLM rates. Keep subscriptions and other fixed fees separate so volume does not hide them.

Is cost per minute enough to compare voice AI systems?

No. A usage rate ignores fixed fees, resolution quality, transfers, repeat contacts, and human cleanup. Compare loaded cost per correctly resolved call.

Which voice AI costs are usually missed?

Include phone numbers, minimum commitments, silence and hold time, retries, failed calls, monitoring, transfers, post-call work, and human escalation.

How do you convert voice AI cost per minute into cost per resolved call?

Add monthly AI spend and human handoff cost, then divide by calls the AI resolved correctly without a person.
