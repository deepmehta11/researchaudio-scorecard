Publisher FAQ

Three rules prevent broken attribution.

How do I recommend ResearchAudio through Beehiiv?

Open Grow → Recommendations, search for ResearchAudio or researchaudio.io, and add it as a free recommendation. New subscribers can then choose ResearchAudio during signup.

Where can I use the ResearchAudio Beehiiv Magic Link?

Only inside a Beehiiv newsletter email. Beehiiv replaces its email placeholder for each recipient and completes the free subscription in one click.

What link should I use on a website or another email platform?

Use the universal tracked starter-kit link. It works on websites, social posts, and email platforms other than Beehiiv.

Can I embed a ResearchAudio AI tool for free?

Yes. Use any of the seventeen source-attributed iframe tools in public articles, documentation, tutorials, courses, and resource pages while keeping the visible credit intact.
