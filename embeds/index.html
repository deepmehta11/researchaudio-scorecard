Publisher FAQ

Before you paste the code.

How do I embed an AI calculator on my website?

Enter your site name, choose a ResearchAudio tool, copy its iframe, and paste it into a custom HTML or embed block on your website.

Which website platforms support the AI tool embeds?

Any platform that accepts iframe or custom HTML blocks can use the widgets, including WordPress, Webflow, Ghost, Beehiiv, and static HTML sites.

Do embedded ResearchAudio tools collect visitor inputs?

No. Checklist answers and calculator inputs stay in the visitor’s browser. ResearchAudio only receives normal page and link attribution data.

Can I embed the AI tools for free?

Yes. The seven widgets are free to embed in public articles, documentation, tutorials, courses, and resource pages as long as the visible ResearchAudio credit remains.

Use conditions

Free to embed. Keep the credit visible.
