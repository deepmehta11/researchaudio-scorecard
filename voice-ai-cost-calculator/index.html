What this voice AI cost calculator computes.

Voice stack cost adds the user-supplied platform, telephony, STT, TTS, and LLM rates. Monthly AI cost is that rate multiplied by connected minutes, plus fixed monthly fees.

Failure burden is unresolved calls multiplied by human cleanup time and loaded hourly cost. This makes handoffs visible instead of treating them as free.

Loaded cost per AI-resolved call divides total AI and handoff spend by calls resolved without a human. It is deliberately stricter than cost per minute or cost per attempted call.

Break-even resolution solves for the AI resolution rate at which loaded cost per AI-resolved call equals the stated human baseline. Validate resolution correctness, repeat contacts, abandonment, and customer outcomes before treating containment as success.

How do you calculate AI voice agent cost?

Add platform, telephony, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and model cost per minute. Multiply by connected minutes, add fixed fees, then add the human work created by unresolved calls.

What is cost per resolved call?

This calculator divides all voice AI and handoff spend by calls the AI resolves without a person. That exposes paid attempts that fail, transfer, or create cleanup work.

Why does resolution rate change voice AI economics?

A lower resolution rate means fewer successful outcomes from the same usage bill and more human escalations. The denominator shrinks while the failure burden grows.

Which fees belong in a voice AI cost model?

Include orchestration, transport, STT, LLM, TTS, fixed subscriptions, phone numbers, capacity, support, and human escalation. Use current invoices or provider rate cards because pricing changes.
