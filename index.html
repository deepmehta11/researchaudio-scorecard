How to read the score

A low score is not a verdict. It is a list of unanswered questions.

0–2

Marketing claim

Interesting enough to watch, not strong enough to plan around.

3–4

Promising, under-proven

Run a bounded test before committing budget, users, or architecture.

5–6

Decision-ready

Enough evidence exists for a real evaluation with known open questions.

7

Evidence-complete

The launch gives an independent team a fair chance to reproduce the result.
